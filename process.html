Idea Assessment & Market Evaluation
Overview

We begin your journey with a comprehensive evaluation of your product concept, assessing its market potential, technical feasibility, and regulatory requirements for Ghana and West African markets. Our food science expertise ensures your idea has a solid foundation for success.

What We Do
  • Free initial consultation to understand your vision and business goals
  • Market potential analysis for Ghana and West African distribution
  • Technical feasibility study based on food science principles
  • Regulatory landscape assessment (FDA, GSA compliance requirements)
  • Competitive analysis and positioning strategy
  • Resource requirement evaluation and cost estimation
  • Development roadmap creation with clear timelines
Key Deliverables
  • Detailed feasibility report with market insights
  • Product development roadmap with milestones
  • Preliminary cost and timeline estimates
  • Regulatory compliance checklist
Product Development & Formulation
Overview

Our comprehensive R&D support transforms your concept into a market-ready product. We handle everything from formulation to packaging design, ensuring compliance with Ghanaian standards and international best practices including GMP, HACCP, and ISO protocols.

What We Do
  • Professional product formulation and recipe development
  • Nutritional analysis and shelf-life testing
  • Sensory evaluation and consumer testing
  • Dehydration services for fruits, vegetables, and herbs (using our solar dehydration plant)
  • Quality control and food safety testing
  • Packaging material selection and design development
  • Professional labeling with nutritional information
  • Brand identity development through Zee Afrika
  • GMP-compliant production protocols
Key Deliverables
  • Finalized product formulation with specifications
  • Complete nutritional and safety test reports
  • Professional packaging and label designs
  • Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
  • Quality control documentation
Market Preparation & Regulatory Compliance
Overview

We simplify the complex certification process, guiding you through Ghana FDA and GSA registration. Our LinkinLabs digital lab access and certification support ensures your product meets all safety, quality, and legal standards for successful market entry.

What We Do
  • Ghana FDA registration and approval facilitation
  • GSA (Ghana Standards Authority) certification support
  • Complete documentation preparation and submission
  • Laboratory testing coordination and management
  • GMP, HACCP, and ISO training for your team
  • Facility inspection preparation and support
  • Regulatory compliance audits and gap analysis
  • Digital lab access through LinkinLabs platform
  • Export documentation for West African markets (if applicable)
Key Deliverables
  • FDA approval certificate and product registration
  • GSA certification documentation
  • Complete regulatory compliance file
  • GMP/HACCP training certificates for staff
  • Market-ready product samples
