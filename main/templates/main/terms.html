2. Scope of Scientific & Consulting Services

Pneuma Food Scientifics provides specialized technical and commercialization advisory services across the food, cosmetics, wellness, and herbal sectors, including:

  • Regulatory Compliance & Coaching: Guidance on product registrations, labeling standards, and audit readiness for the Ghana Food and Drugs Authority (FDA) and Ghana Standards Authority (GSA).
  • Product Formulation & R&D: Standardized recipe development, shelf-life testing oversight, and sensory evaluation.
  • Factory & Equipment Layouts: Technical facility layout advisory, hazard mitigation, and code-compliant equipment sourcing.
  • Commercial Food Dehydration: Commercial processing and dehydration of plant and agricultural products.
  • Laboratory Testing Access: Analytical, microbiological, and chemical test facilitation via LinkinLabs and accredited laboratory partners.
  • Business Advisory & Coaching: Drafting business plans, financial projections, and operational quality management systems (HACCP).

B. Deposit Structure for Custom Deliverables

  • Quality Assurance Manuals & Custom Documentation: Execution requires an upfront, non-refundable commitment deposit of 50% of the total quoted fee. The remaining balance of 50% is due immediately upon delivery of the initial draft for review.

C. Commercial Dehydration Fees

  • Commercial dehydration services are charged based on machine hours (ranging between GHS 10 to GHS 25 per hour) plus labor fees (starting from GHS 100 per day per staff member).

D. Third-Party & Statutory Fees
