Who We Are

Pneuma Food Scientifics (PFS), established in 2018, is Ghana's most promising product innovation and entrepreneurial training hub. We transform ideas into market-ready products for startups and SMEs in the food, cosmetics, and herbal sectors, serving Ghanaian market. Our comprehensive, science-driven approach fosters sustainable business and community transformation.

Our Vision

To become West Africa’s leading product development and compliance hub for food, cosmetics, wellness, and herbal enterprises.

Our Mission

To provide scientifically grounded solutions, regulatory systems, and sustainable production support that transform Ghanaian ideas into marketable and impactful products. We strive to build lasting partnerships, foster innovation, and maintain the highest standards of integrity in everything we do.

PFS: A Story of Passion and Impact

Zeenatu Suglo Adams is not only a food scientist but a visionary leader who transformed her challenges into a purposeful solution

After years of witnessing passionate entrepreneurs struggle with product development, regulatory barriers, and lack of quality infrastructure, she resolved to build what was missing: a holistic support system for SMEs in Ghana. Pneuma Food Scientifics (PFS) was established out of a deep commitment to excellence, access, and transformation

Zeenatu founded PFS with her scientific knowledge, persistence, and a strong belief in the potential of African brands. Today, she leads a multidisciplinary team that supports hundreds of businesses through science, compliance, and innovation

PFS's journey is a testament to Zeenatu's vision of empowering entrepreneurs, fostering innovation, and driving sustainable growth in Ghana's food and wellness sectors. Her leadership continues to inspire a new generation of African innovators.

"This is what sustains me, what brings me joy, and what defines my legacy. I am proud of the work I do."
- Zeenatu Suglo Adams
