How long does the FDA certification process take? FDA & GSA

The timeline depends on how prepared you are:

  1. FDA will typically call you 6 - 10 weeks after we submit your documents to them.
  2. If you have all required documents ready, PFS can submit to the FDA office within a maximum of 2 weeks.
  3. Express service is also available for faster processing.
What documents do I need to provide for FDA certification? FDA & GSA

For food / herbal / pharmaceutical businesses:

  • Food Handlers Card (from your district assembly bring a passport photo)
  • Product lab test results (PFS can assist)
  • Product label (PFS can help)
  • Product pictures
  • Business Certificate (PFS can assist)
  • Product samples exactly as they will be sold

For food joints / eateries / sit-and-dine businesses:

  • Food Handlers Card
  • List of your recipes
  • Steps in making the recipes (PFS can help)
  • Business Certificate

For cosmetic & household chemical businesses:

  • Product label, product pictures
  • Business Certificate
  • Product samples as they will be sold

What are the official FDA local registration charges? FDA & GSA

Food Processing Industry:

  • Cottage Industry (1–3 persons): Product Reg GHC 300 + Facility Reg GHC 100
  • Small Scale (4–10 persons): Product Reg GHC 600 + Facility Reg GHC 100
  • Medium Scale (10+ persons): Product Reg GHC 900 + Facility Reg GHC 200

Cosmetics / Household Chemicals:

  • Cottage: Product Reg GHC 300 + Facility Reg GHC 100
  • Small Scale: Product Reg GHC 600 + Facility Reg GHC 100
  • Medium Scale: Product Reg GHC 750 + Facility Reg GHC 200

Imported Products:

  • Cosmetics: $177 per product + warehouse licensing GHC 240–800
  • Food products: GHC 750 per product + warehouse licensing GHC 240–800
  • Veterinary drugs: $600 per product + GHC 500 warehouse
  • Food supplements: $500 per product + GHC 500 warehouse

Dehydration

What must be on my product label to comply with FDA requirements? Packaging

FDA's General Labelling Requirements for pre-packaged food include:

  • Product ID: Brand name & generic name
  • Quantity: Net weight, net volume, drained weight (if applicable)
  • Ingredients: Specific names and E-numbers where applicable
  • Dates: Date of manufacture, best before date, use-by date
  • Traceability: Batch number, lot number, country of origin
  • Manufacturer info: Name, full address, Ghana Post Digital Address
  • Usage: Directions for use, storage and handling instructions
  • Language: English only, legible, printed in indelible ink
  • Additional: Both primary and secondary packaging must comply; documentary evidence for nutrition claims; plant source for vegetable oils
